Can an online tutorial be as effective as a physical tutorial?

Yes, 100%. An online tutorial can be just as effective as a physical tutorial, and in some cases even more convenient.

With physical tutorials, you have to spend money and time getting to the venue. You may also deal with noise, distractions, or sitting far from the tutor where you can't hear properly.

With EOT, you can learn from wherever you are and access your classes online. And if you miss a class, you don't miss the lesson completely. You can go back, replay the voice explanations, study the materials and review the class again until you understand it.

So you get the flexibility to learn at your convenience, while still having access to structured classes, practice questions, mock exams and support. The key isn't whether the tutorial is physical or online. It's having the right system and actually following through with it.
